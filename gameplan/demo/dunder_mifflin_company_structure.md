## Company Culture Notes

Dunder Mifflin Scranton has a unique workplace culture characterized by:
- Close-knit team dynamics
- Informal communication style
- Strong emphasis on personal relationships
- Quirky office traditions and events
- Focus on both productivity and fun
- Regional paper industry expertise
- Family-like atmosphere among long-term employees

This structure provides a realistic foundation for demonstrating Gameplan's team collaboration, project management, and discussion features in a familiar and entertaining context.
